About Swat International Hospital

Serving the People of Swat with Compassion & Clinical Excellence

Located conveniently near the Old NADRA Office in Mingora, Swat International Hospital was established to bring comprehensive, ethical, and affordable tertiary-level healthcare closer to families across Swat, Shangla, Buner, and Upper & Lower Dir.

From safe motherhood and neonatal care to minimally invasive laparoscopic surgery, cardiology, and internal medicine, every patient receives personalized attention in a clean, healing environment.
